## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Follow a coin through changing measurements and retained observations. The user-pinned watch/table/chart arrangement is the surface's authority.

**OWN-WORLD:** Graphite fields, flat bordered panels, compact sans typography, cyan actions and explicit green/rose direction labels. Identity follows the approved concept and the extracted DESIGN.md cascade.

**STORY:** Scan activity, select or watch a symbol, inspect closed-candle evidence and reasons, then enter manual risk assumptions. History shows what happened; current labels show what remains verified.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Header and search/filter strip precede the watchlist at left, a broad central coin table and selected-coin chart/details at right. Observation events sit immediately below the left workspace. Primary search and watch actions are visible; risk remains a deliberate disclosure.

**FORM:** User-pinned generated concept, implemented directly in the existing panel. No random direction selection was used; no seed key exists. The concept's illustrative Models tab and example rating claims yield to the user's later four-page and explainable-activity instructions.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
